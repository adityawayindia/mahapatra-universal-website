Before You Apply

A few things to keep in mind.

Keep your resume current

Include your recent roles, key projects, qualifications and certifications. A clear, concise resume helps us match you faster.

One application is enough

We keep every profile on file and consider it for all suitable openings — there is no need to apply more than once in six months.

Prefer email?

Send your resume to hr@mahapatrauniversal.com with your area of interest in the subject line.

Beware of recruitment fraud

We never ask candidates for money at any stage of hiring. Official communication only comes from an @mahapatrauniversal.com address.

Candidate FAQs

Questions, answered.

How do I know which roles are currently open?

We hire on a rolling basis across our businesses. Submit your application with your area of interest, and our talent team will reach out when a role matching your profile opens up.

Can I apply to more than one area?

Select the area that best matches your core expertise. If you are open to other areas, mention them in your cover note — we review every profile against openings across the group.

How long does the hiring process take?

We aim to review profiles within two weeks. Interview stages and timelines depend on the role and business, and we will keep you informed at each step.

Do you offer internships?

Yes. Students can apply using the form above. Please mention your course, institution and available dates in the cover note.

Will I be charged any fee during recruitment?

No. Mahapatra Universal Limited never charges candidates any fee at any stage. If you receive such a request, please do not pay and report it to hr@mahapatrauniversal.com.

How is my personal data used?

Your details are used only for recruitment purposes and handled in line with our Privacy Policy. You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by writing to our HR team.

Our Legacy

From strategic vision to a multifaceted global institution.

Over sixteen years of disciplined enterprise stewardship, Preeti Mahapatra has built and managed high-impact operating companies across manufacturing, technology, hospitality, and financial services — turning underperforming industry assets into market leaders through active operational restructuring.

Today, Mahapatra Universal Limited operates with unwavering governance, combining local industry agility with international financial acumen across Asia, Europe, and the Americas.

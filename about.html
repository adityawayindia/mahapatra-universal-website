About Mahapatra Universal

Building resilient enterprises, empowering global economies.

A diversified multi-industry conglomerate founded by Preeti Mahapatra, spanning four signature brands and eleven business verticals across seven countries.

Our Legacy

From strategic vision to a multifaceted global institution.

Preeti Mahapatra has orchestrated strategic investments across Engineering, Information Technology, Sports & Media, Hospitality, Solar Power and Renewable Energy, Gems & Jewellery, and Mining. Under her stewardship, the group consolidated and acquired six companies into a single division, going on to champion two further acquisitions while restructuring operations to drive cost efficiency and profitability.

Today, Mahapatra Universal Limited operates with unwavering governance, combining local industry agility with international financial acumen across operations in over seven countries and three continents.

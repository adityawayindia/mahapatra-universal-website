Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Mahapatra Universal Limited collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information across our websites and services.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Mahapatra Universal Limited ("Mahapatra Universal," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect through our websites, enquiry forms, and related services, how we use and safeguard that information, and the choices available to you.

By using our websites or submitting information through our forms, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you interact with our websites or contact our teams:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, phone number, and company name, provided through our enquiry and contact forms.
  • Message content including the subject and details of any enquiry, proposal, or request you submit to us.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically through standard web server logs and cookies.
  • Recruitment information such as resumes, cover letters, and related details, if you apply for a role or contact our Human Resources team.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries, proposals, and requests submitted through our contact or enquiry forms.
  • Route your enquiry to the appropriate business vertical or departmental desk.
  • Communicate with you regarding partnerships, investments, career opportunities, or media relations.
  • Improve the content, functionality, and security of our websites.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Group entities and business verticals within Mahapatra Universal Limited, where relevant to your enquiry.
  • Service providers who assist in operating our websites, provided they agree to keep your information confidential.
  • Regulatory or governmental authorities where required by applicable law.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance browsing experience, remember preferences, and analyse site traffic. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, doing so may affect certain website functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

9. Third-Party Links

Our websites may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such external sites and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
